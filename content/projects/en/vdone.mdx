## Overview

VDONE is an integrated social networking and e-commerce ecosystem connecting end consumers, wholesale suppliers, manufacturers, and logistics partners. The infrastructure comprises cross-platform mobile applications, dedicated administrative web portals, and proprietary digital wallet payment systems.

## Key Features

### Mobile Applications (iOS & Android)
- **Social Networking & Community**:
  - Biometric eKYC user identity verification.
  - Interactive live streaming and short-form video feeds.
  - Rich social newsfeed, user comments, and sharing.
  - End-to-end encrypted messaging and peer-to-peer VoIP calls.
- **Commerce & Financials**:
  - MarketHome online retail shopping catalog.
  - Multi-tier in-app wallet (Coins & dOne balance).
  - Order checkout, invoice generation, and status tracking.
  - In-app merchant storefront management.

### Web Management Portals
- **Supply Chain Management**:
  - Supplier (NCC) Portal for product catalog ingestion and pricing.
  - Manufacturer (NSX) Portal for manufacturing volume and batch tracking.
  - Vstore operations and inventory warehouse management.
- **Corporate Operations & Governance**:
  - Logistics coordination and dispatch console.
  - Automated ledger and accounting reconciliation.
  - Role-based user access controls and real-time analytical reporting.

## Technical Architecture

- **Mobile Client**: Flutter cross-platform framework delivering high-performance 60fps animations, WebRTC video streaming, and real-time push notifications.
- **Backend Architecture**: NestJS microservices orchestration utilizing message brokers for decoupled service-to-service communication.
- **Core Microservices**: Authentication/RBAC, User Profile, Payment Processing, Order Management, Media Streaming, and Push Notification workers.
- **Web Frontend**: Next.js administrative dashboards and supplier management portals styled with Tailwind CSS.

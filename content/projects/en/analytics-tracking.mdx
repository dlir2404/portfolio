## Overview

In global e-commerce platforms, merchants require instant, precise visibility into traffic sources, add-to-cart conversions, net profit, and advertising attribution. Traditional relational databases fail under the massive write volumes of holiday traffic spikes and complex aggregation queries.

As the **Development Owner** for the Analytics & Tracking domain, I designed and stabilized a real-time data ingestion and analytical processing pipeline capable of ingesting millions of storefront tracking events and aggregating revenue and profit metrics with sub-second dashboard query latency.

## Architecture & Data Flow

The system employs a decoupled, streaming-first architecture:

```text
[ Global Shopper Browser ]
           │
           ▼
 [Edge Tracking Gateway (:8080)] ──► Ultra-low latency payload validation & geo-enrichment
           │
           ▼
   [Storefront Service (:3006)] ──► Session resolution (sb_ctx with 'u:' user prefix)
           │
           ▼
     [Apache Kafka Bus] ──► Partitioned topic streaming & backpressure buffer
           │
           ▼
[ClickHouse Columnar Storage] ──► events_1h_all_columns (Vectorized parallel aggregations)
           │
           ▲
[Distributed Celery Workers] ──► Asynchronous profit, COGS & order attribution engine
```

### 1. Ingestion at the Edge
- **Edge Gateway (:8080):** High-concurrency reverse proxy terminating tracking beacons with negligible impact on buyer page-load performance.
- **Storefront Context (:3006):** Preserves cross-domain session cookies and injects deterministic session tokens (`sb_ctx` with `u:` user prefix) to unify anonymous browsing with authenticated checkouts.

### 2. High-Throughput Streaming & Columnar Storage
- **Apache Kafka:** Serves as a fault-tolerant buffer decoupling traffic surges from downstream analytical ingestion.
- **ClickHouse Cluster (`events_1h_all_columns`):** Stores append-only raw events with aggressive compression. Columnar partitioning enables parallel aggregations across hundreds of millions of rows in milliseconds.

### 3. Distributed Profit & Metric Aggregations
- Configured specialized **Celery task pools** to continuously compute complex profit formulas (gross revenue minus cost of goods sold, shipping fees, dynamic currency conversions, and gateway charges).
- Real-time event streaming reconciles abandoned checkouts and completed orders into merchant dashboard cards.

## Key Engineering Achievements

### Resolving Data Skew & Tracking Discrepancies
- Prior to stabilization, tracking reports suffered from subtle metric discrepancies between raw gateway logs and dashboard summaries.
- Conducted deep forensic analysis across the entire ingestion pipeline:
  - Isolated and resolved **7 core root-cause data skew clusters** (time-zone offsets, duplicate webhook delivery, session token stripping).
  - Fixed **11 critical tracking and aggregation bugs**.
  - Verified stability through end-to-end QE regression suites and live production audit runs.

### Performance & Scalability Gains
- Achieved **99.99% event ingestion reliability** during peak sales periods.
- Reduced analytical query latency from 8+ seconds down to under **350ms**, enabling real-time live dashboard updates for merchants.

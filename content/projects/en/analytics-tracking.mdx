## Introduction

In modern high-velocity e-commerce, real-time data accuracy is the heartbeat of merchant success. Sellers scaling five-figure daily advertising budgets cannot afford to wait until midnight to determine whether an ad campaign is profitable or bleeding money. They need immediate answers: Exactly how much gross revenue was generated in the last hour? After deducting product costs, shipping, and payment gateway fees, what is the exact **Net Profit** remaining? At which step in the checkout flow are prospective buyers dropping off?

From a merchant-facing perspective, the product experience builds upon the familiar, proven reporting workflow of the earlier ShopBase analytics ecosystem (Profit Dashboards, Funnels, Sales Reports, Live View, and Traffic Sources).

However, transition to the new platform generation required a **complete architectural redesign of the event capture and processing engine**: Instead of relying on traditional client-side JavaScript SDKs (which are prone to browser ad-blockers, tracking prevention, and page-load overhead), the system shifted to direct telemetry capture via **Backend Hooks and Edge infrastructure**. This redesign ensures bulletproof event delivery, zero data loss, and pristine data accuracy while preserving a clean, familiar reporting interface for merchants.

## Core Product Capabilities

### 1. Real-Time Net Profit & Performance Dashboard
- Instant metric refresh the second an order is confirmed.
- Automatically factors in all variable costs: Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), warehouse fulfillment, dynamic international currency conversions, and merchant gateway transaction surcharges.
- In-depth operational KPIs: Total Orders, Average Order Profit (AOP), Average Order Items (AOI), and Customer Retention Rates.

### 2. Comprehensive 7-Step Conversion Funnel Tracking
- Visually maps the shopper journey through each critical conversion milestone:  
  `View product (VP) ➔ Add to cart (AC) ➔ Reach checkout (RC) ➔ Fill email (FE) ➔ Fill shipping address (FS) ➔ Fill phone (FP) ➔ Purchase (PU)`.
- Pinpoints exact step-by-step drop-off rates with granular daily and country breakdowns.
- Tracks daily conversion rate fluctuations over time (`Conversion rate over time`).

### 3. Traffic Sources & Multi-Touch Attribution
- Accurately attributes orders to their originating promotional channels: Direct, Referral, Search (Google), or specific paid campaigns.
- Flexible deep-dive dimensions: group by Channel, UTM Source, UTM Medium, Source / Medium, UTM Campaign, or UTM Term.
- Empowers merchants to double down on winning campaigns and trim underperforming ad spend with total confidence.

- **Sales Reports by Product:** Itemized sales table tracking product views, add-to-cart volumes, cart conversion rates (`%ATC / View product`), and completed checkouts.
- **Real-Time Live View:** Dashboard auto-refreshing every 10 seconds, displaying active visitors right now, current sessions, real-time revenue, and a global interactive live transactions map.

## My Role & Ownership

Serving as the **Sole Development Owner** for the Analytics & Tracking domain:

- **Redesigning the Event Model & Pipeline:** Spearheaded the transition from client-side JS SDK tracking to robust server-side capture using **Backend Hooks and Edge processing**, guaranteeing zero event drops from browser ad-blockers and eliminating tracking script overhead on shopper storefronts.
- **Resolving Metric Discrepancies:** Headed the forensic investigation into historic reporting discrepancies (such as cross-border timezone skews, duplicate webhook deliveries, and session attribution loss during checkout redirects). Successfully realigned reporting metrics to match real-world bank balances with 100% precision.
- **High-Traffic Reliability:** Ensured dashboard uptime and sub-second query responsiveness during major global flash sales (Black Friday, Cyber Monday) under tens of millions of concurrent requests.
- **Quality Engineering & Verification:** Partnered closely with the QE team to author exhaustive test suites, certifying that every financial calculation displayed to merchants is battle-tested against edge cases.

## Business Impact

- **Rapid Decision-Making:** Sellers can scale profitable ad angles or pause losing ad sets within minutes based on true net profit numbers rather than vanity metrics.
- **Unshakable Merchant Trust:** Eliminated discrepancies between reported platform metrics and actual banking payouts, establishing foundational trust for the entire e-commerce ecosystem.

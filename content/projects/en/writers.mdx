## Introduction

During my engineering internship at **True Platform**, I designed and developed an intelligent **SEO Content Optimization Suite**.  
The application tackles common daily pain points faced by digital marketers, content strategists, and copywriters:

- Uncertainty about whether a written article adheres to the latest search engine ranking guidelines.  
- Tedious hours spent manually auditing competitor search results and mapping keywords.  
- The need to rapidly draft and iterate on SEO-friendly prose without sacrificing tone or originality.

The product was engineered atop an **in-house PHP framework**, with a **Vanilla JavaScript + jQuery** frontend, seamlessly integrating specialized SEO data APIs and generative AI models.

## My Role & Responsibilities

- Gathered domain requirements and mapped the entire product lifecycle from ideation to release.  
- Developed the **frontend**: rich text editor, real-time SEO scoring panel, keyword density heatmaps, and competitor comparison matrices.  
- Engineered the **backend**: integrated OpenAI API, DataForSEO, Google Search Console API, and asynchronous web scrapers.  
- Optimized performance through intelligent API response caching and message queues for asynchronous bulk copy generation.

## Key Capabilities

### 1. Real-Time SEO Scoring
- Live evaluation of title length, meta description, heading structure (H1-H4), keyword density, and article depth.  
- Displays an **aggregate SEO Health Score** alongside actionable improvement suggestions directly within the editor.

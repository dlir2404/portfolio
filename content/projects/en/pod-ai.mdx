## Overview

In the Print-on-Demand (POD) e-commerce industry, customer personalization (custom hair colors, hairstyles, dog/cat breeds, outfits, and names) represents the highest conversion driver. However, manually assembling multi-layer PSD files into production-ready print artworks and photorealistic mockups creates massive operational bottlenecks.

**POD AI** is an enterprise-grade automated artwork generation and rendering pipeline. It bridges generative AI swatching models, multi-layer vector compositing, and distributed rendering workers on AWS EKS to generate high-resolution print files and web-ready store mockups in seconds.

## System Architecture & Workflow

The pipeline operates as a multi-stage asynchronous state machine:

```text
[ Merchant / Buyer Saves Campaign ]
               │
               ▼
   [/save-campaign Trigger]
               │
               ▼
     [pbase_prepare_mockup] ──► Asset validation & layer parameter injection
               │
               ▼
[eks_pod_render_artwork_campaign] ──► High-DPI vector artwork synthesis (300 DPI)
               │
               ▼
    [eks_pod_render_mockup] ──► Photorealistic lighting, displacement & apparel warp
               │
               ▼
         [sb_product] ──► Automated variant publishing to live store
```

### 1. Smart Swatching & Clipart Compositing
- Implemented strict visual companion constraints: Companion attributes (e.g., Hair Style + Hair Color) require deterministic picture-type swatches paired with body and outfit cliparts.
- Dynamic asset mapping resolves hundreds of layer combinations into a cohesive printable canvas without visual artifacts or clipping.

### 2. Distributed Rendering on AWS EKS
- Decoupled CPU-intensive graphic rendering into dedicated worker pools on Kubernetes (EKS).
- Configured dedicated queue routing (`CELERY_TASK_ROUTES`) to separate light metadata processing from multi-gigabyte PSD render jobs, guaranteeing near-zero queue starvation for active merchants.
- High-availability asset synchronization with S3 storage (`assets-pbase`) featuring local development fallbacks.

### 3. LLM Observability & Guardrails with Langfuse
- Integrated **Langfuse** for real-time prompt telemetry, latency profiling, and token cost tracking.
- Implemented robust Basic Authentication and custom User-Agent proxies to eliminate Cloudflare 1010 blocking issues on headless worker nodes.

## Key Engineering Challenges & Solutions

### Mitigating Pricing Race Conditions
- During rapid campaign save flows, pricing recalculations occasionally conflicted with background variant creation.
- **Solution:** Implemented deterministic synchronization checks and network-idle timeouts before finalizing product variants, eliminating intermittent price-mismatch regressions on live storefronts.

### High-DPI Rendering at Scale
- Generating large 300-DPI print files consumed substantial container memory.
- **Solution:** Streamed asset chunks directly from S3, applied lazy layer evaluation, and introduced automated worker autoscaling triggered by queue backlog thresholds.

## Impact & Business Value

- **Render Time:** Cut end-to-end personalization and mockup rendering from minutes to under 8 seconds.
- **Throughput:** Capable of processing thousands of personalized apparel and mug campaigns concurrently during peak holiday sales.
- **Operational Efficiency:** Zero manual designer intervention required for customer-personalized orders.

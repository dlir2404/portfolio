## Introduction

In Print-on-Demand (POD) commerce, personalized products are among the strongest revenue drivers. A hoodie showing a group of friends with their own hairstyles, or a mug showing a customer next to their dog with both names printed, sells at a clear premium over generic merchandise, because buyers value items that reflect their own story.

Behind each personalized listing, however, is a demanding manual setup process that every POD merchant has to go through.

---

## The Problem: Slow and Error-Prone Manual Configuration

Launching a personalized product requires the merchant to configure a large number of visual assets and conditional combinations by hand.

### Example: A "Best Friends & Pets" Campaign
Consider a t-shirt design featuring **two friends and one dog**:
- **Character 1:** 25 hairstyles × 8 hair colors = 200 hair cliparts, plus body poses, outfits, and skin tones, which add hundreds more image layers.
- **Character 2:** The same structure again, adding a further 250 or more assets.
- **Pet:** To cover common requests, the merchant needs at least 40 dog breeds, each in 3 to 4 coat colors, which comes to **more than 150 pet cliparts**.
- **Custom text:** Input fields for each person's name, the pet's name, and a short quote.

**In total, a single campaign can contain between 600 and more than 1,000 individual assets and layer combinations.**

### Limitations of the Manual Process
1. **Several hours per product:** Merchants sliced layers in Photoshop, exported hundreds of transparent PNG files, uploaded them one by one, and named each asset manually.
2. **Complex conditional rules:** Hundreds of rules had to be configured by hand, for example: *"If the buyer selects short wavy hair, show these 8 color swatches; if the buyer selects a Golden Retriever, show only golden and cream coat colors."*
3. **High risk of error:** A single mislabeled layer or misconfigured rule could produce the wrong print, such as a different hair color from the one ordered or a misaligned layer. Each defective print meant a refund and a loss of customer trust.
4. **Missed trends:** When a design started trending on TikTok or Etsy, the time spent on setup delayed the launch and left the opportunity to competitors.

---

## The Solution

**POD AI (AI-Personalized Campaign)** turns hours of manual setup into a guided, AI-driven workflow.

The system connects the full journey, from the merchant's setup to the buyer's purchase, in a single continuous flow:

```text
[ 1. UPLOAD ARTWORK ]      [ 2. CHOOSE MODEL ]         [ 3. EDIT WITH PROMPTS ]    [ 4. PUBLISH & PRINT ]
Detect characters     -->  Details only or        -->  Natural-language       -->  Buyer live preview
and generate options       Count & details (<=7)       layout editing              300 DPI print file
```

### 1. Artwork Upload and Character Detection
- The merchant uploads the master design file (PNG, JPG, or JPEG with a transparent background).
- The AI analyzes the composition and **detects the characters** it contains: people, pets, or objects.
- The merchant can adjust the number of characters (`Set character count`) or add a character manually (`Add Custom`).

### 2. Personalization Models
The platform supports two personalization models:
- **Details only:** The same detail options (hairstyle, skin tone, pet breed) apply to every character of the same type.
- **Count and details:** The buyer chooses how many characters appear (for example, one, two, or three pets) and customizes each character individually, with up to seven characters per design.

### 3. Automatic Options, Swatches, and Conditional Logic
- Supported input types: `Picture Choice`, `Color Swatch`, `Select Option`, `Text Input`, and `Number Tiles`.
- **Automatic conditional logic:** The system infers pairing rules between attributes. For example, selecting a cat breed limits the color swatches to the coat colors of that breed.
- Merchants can generate new cliparts and images with AI directly in the configuration screen.

### 4. Prompt-Based Layout Editing and Storefront Preview
- **Edit with prompt:** Instead of dragging elements manually, the merchant describes the change in natural language (for example, *"position the characters closer together"* or *"move the pet to the right"*), and the AI adjusts the layout accordingly.
- **Improve Composition and Fix Characters:** The AI rebalances the composition in a single step.
- **Preview storefront:** The merchant sees exactly what buyers will interact with before publishing the campaign.

### 5. Product Mockups and Campaign Launch
- The design is applied across multiple base products, such as t-shirts, mugs, hoodies, and canvas prints.
- **Remove background when generating preview:** Backgrounds are removed during preview generation so that the artwork looks natural on the product mockup.
- The merchant sets the title, description, and pricing, then publishes the campaign.

### 6. Buyer Personalization and Automated Fulfillment
- Buyers choose hairstyles, pets, and names on the product page and see a **live preview** on the product mockup.
- After checkout, the system composes the selected layers into a **print-ready 300 DPI file** and sends it to the print facility without manual layer assembly.

## My Role & Contributions

Working in a 3-member engineering team, I took part in the **full flow end to end**: seller setup → campaign launch → buyer personalization → checkout and print fulfillment, all the way to the production release.

- **AI Setup Pipeline:** Built the generation steps behind seller setup: artwork detection, option-tree and clipart generation, an LLM check-and-repair loop that guarantees every layout prompt has the slots it needs, placeholder layouts, and an AI step that decides which cliparts become color swatches.
- **Intelligent Swatching & Conditional Rules:** Built the attribute-pairing logic that connects visual silhouettes with color swatches, freeing sellers from manually scripting hundreds of conditional rules.
- **Resilient Image Generation:** Added a multi-provider fallback chain for buyer-side artwork generation, so that personalization keeps working when one AI provider slows down or fails.
- **Seller & Buyer Experience:** Worked across the seller configuration console in the admin dashboard and the live customization widget on storefronts.
- **Launch & Fulfillment:** Connected campaign launch to the print pipeline, so that each personalized order produces a print-ready 300 DPI file automatically.
- **Production Quality:** Wrote end-to-end test suites covering the full campaign launch flow (2D, 3D, and the complete UI flow) and the clipart-reuse feature.

---

## Business Value

- **Hours of Setup Become an AI Workflow:** Instead of hand-building complex option sets, sellers upload artwork and the AI generates the options, swatches, and layouts, greatly shortening the time to launch a personalized campaign.
- **Fewer Manual Errors:** Generated options and automated print files remove most of the misaligned layers, wrong hair colors, and mismatched names that come from manual setup.
- **Personalization Buyers Can See:** Shoppers preview their own personalized design before buying, instead of guessing from a static mockup.

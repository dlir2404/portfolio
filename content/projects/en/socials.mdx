## Introduction

**Socials** is a social media publishing suite built on the **"write once, publish everywhere"** paradigm.  
The application empowers marketers to compose, draft, schedule, and synchronize posts across major networks: **Facebook, Instagram, Threads, X (Twitter), and LinkedIn** from a single intuitive dashboard.  
The system leverages **background queues, cron scheduler workers, and full-text search with Apache Solr** to ensure high throughput and reliability. The solution is running live in **production**.

## My Role & Responsibilities

- Engineered the complete system with the company's internal PHP and JS framework.  
- Designed the backend asynchronous job processing pipeline for delayed publishing and automated metric syncing.  
- Integrated official platform APIs (Facebook Graph API, Instagram API, Threads, X API v2, LinkedIn Marketing API).  
- Integrated **Apache Solr** for sub-second full-text search across tens of thousands of historic posts and marketing campaigns.  
- **Handcrafted the entire interactive drag-and-drop Calendar View from scratch using vanilla HTML, CSS, JS, and jQuery without relying on third-party calendar libraries.**

## Introduction

In modern short-form video commerce (TikTok Shop, Affiliate Marketing), high-converting video creative is the lifeblood of revenue. Content creators and e-commerce sellers face a persistent dilemma:
- Producing fresh videos from scratch is slow, labor-intensive, and requires expensive models and studio spaces.
- Finding a competitor's winning video and re-uploading it directly triggers TikTok's aggressive **Duplicate Content Algorithms**, immediately suppressing distribution to 0 views or causing channel suspension.

**StackVid** solves this friction through an autonomous end-to-end pipeline: scraping competitor videos that have verified shopping cart attachments, processing them through the **T2T (TikTok-to-TikTok / Video-to-Video) AI Engine** to transform the actor, background, and lip-synced script while **strictly preserving 100% of the apparel/product being sold**, and automatically scheduling publishing with attached TikTok Shop product showcase anchors.

## Core Focus: T2T AI Video Engine & TikTok Shop Automation

### 1. Competitor Channel Scraping & Shopping Cart Telemetry
- **Showcase E-Commerce Mining:** Automatically tracks target competitor accounts (e.g. `@sieu.tichcuc`), continuously indexing their video catalog.
- **Cart Attachment Classification:** Accurately distinguishes videos that feature e-commerce product links (e.g. `Anchor Product #867635`) versus generic organic posts without carts.
- **Source Telemetry Extraction:** Downloads original clean HD MP4 files without watermarks, analyzes engagement metrics (views, likes), and extracts native audio transcripts (WebVTT) to serve as source prompts.

### 2. Master Anchor Architecture & Garment Preservation
To eliminate 100% of TikTok's duplicate content penalties, the pipeline enforces a dual-anchor strategy:
- **Actor & Environment Swapping:** Replaces the competitor's presenter and messy real-world background with an assigned digital persona (e.g., persona `Dũng`) within a pristine, minimalist studio setting.
- **Strict Garment Constraint:** AI identifies the commercial apparel item and locks down product geometry and details (`washed blue denim jacket, regular fit, metal buttons, chest flap pockets... - Keep product unchanged`). From fabric texture, wash tone, to pocket flaps and buttons, the garment remains authentic to what buyers actually receive.

### 3. AI Script Restructuring & Lip-Sync Rhythm Engine
- **5-Segment Direct Response Formula:** Slices source videos into semantic chunks (`#0 HOOK ➔ #1 FEATURE ➔ #2 FEATURE ➔ #3 BENEFIT ➔ #4 CTA`), generating a refreshed, copyright-free sales script highlighting key product benefits.
- **Pacing Normalization:** Constrains script density to **25–36 words per chunk**, precisely matching the visual duration of each 10-second segment (240 frames at 24 fps) so that synthesized voiceover naturally aligns with the character's lip movements.

### 4. Quality Consistency Scoring & Real Finished Video Output
- **Automated QA Metrics:** Evaluates every rendered chunk against AI consistency thresholds: Facial consistency (`Face: 78%`) and Garment consistency (`Clothing: 80%`).
- **Autonomous Stitching & Audio Remix:** Seamlessly concatenates all 5 completed segments into a unified 9:16 vertical video and remixes the audio track with native Vietnamese vocal cadences.

### 5. Automated Scheduling & TikTok Shop Cart Attachment
- **Autonomous Factory Watch Mode:** Toggling `Watch: ON` automatically pulls new uploads from monitored channels into the processing queue without manual supervision.
- **Auto-Publishing with E-Commerce Anchors:** Deploys rendered videos during optimal engagement windows (`08:00 - 22:30`) to the destination storefront channel (e.g., `Thời trang Kudo`), automatically binding the designated TikTok Shop showcase product link for seamless affiliate monetization.

## Supporting Infrastructure & Features

In addition to the core T2T engine, StackVid incorporates essential utilities for multi-account operational safety:

- **TikTok Streak Keeper:** Automates daily direct messaging to preserve account engagement streaks (`🔥`). Employs a smart "End-of-Day Rescue" mode (22:30) that skips execution if accounts already interacted, alongside a 0–15 minute randomized delay (jitter) to prevent anti-bot heuristics from flagging accounts.
- **Instant VietQR Top-Up:** Reconciles banking transfers via automated QR codes (SePay), updating credit wallets in 5–15 seconds without administrative intervention.
- **NestJS Monorepo & gRPC Microservices:** Decouples public API gateways from heavy compute workers via binary gRPC for minimal inter-service latency.
- **Selective Build Optimization & Parallel Deploy Pipelines:**
  - *Change-Aware Selective Builds:* The monorepo hosts multiple microservices (`api-gateway`, `tiktok-service`, `flow-service`, `flow-worker`) and frontend packages (`dashboard`, `admin`, `landing`, `docs`). Leveraging `pnpm --filter` and targeted Nest CLI compilation, CI/CD pipelines compile only the exact modified services instead of rebuilding the full repository, slashing build and deployment times by over 75%.
  - *Multi-Stage Docker & Parallel Image Packaging:* Dedicated Dockerfiles (`Dockerfile.gateway`, `Dockerfile.tiktok`, `Dockerfile.flow-*`) share cached base layers and package container images concurrently across distributed build runners.
  - *Parallel Chunk Rendering Pipeline:* Sliced video segments within the T2T workflow are distributed simultaneously across worker pools, reducing end-to-end rendering latency from several minutes down to under 40 seconds.
- **Atomic Two-Phase Credits Engine:** Employs atomic SQL deductions and an interceptor pattern to hold funds in escrow and automatically refund 100% if third-party APIs fail.
- **Dedicated Sticky Proxies:** Binds each connected social account to an isolated residential proxy IP and sandboxed cookie vault.
